## The Details

Single Filter is the simplest post maker ever created. It is the antidote to bloated, overly complex design tools. Built for creators who just want to get their thoughts out beautifully, it removes the friction of endless menus, layer adjustments, and tedious resizing.

Instead of spamming buttons and dragging items around to make them look good together, Single Filter handles the layout logic automatically. Just add your content, click a few buttons, and your post is instantly ready for social media.

## The Features

- **Zero-Clutter Interface:** Only the tools you actually need.
- **Auto-Alignment:** No more guessing if your text is centered.
- **One-Click Styling:** Instantly apply cohesive themes without manual tweaking.
- **Fast Export:** Download your perfect post in seconds.
- **Fully Responsive:** Create beautiful posts directly from your phone or desktop.
- **Dark & Light Mode:** Easy on the eyes, whenever you work.
