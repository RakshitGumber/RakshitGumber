# Competence

Competence is usually invisible. It shows up as the quiet confidence that a page will load, the links will work, and the copy will make sense the second time someone reads it.

## What I try to preserve

- Small surfaces that are easy to understand.
- A path from idea to shipped work without extra ceremony.
- Enough structure that the next change does not feel like a gamble.

The work here is intentionally plain. If the structure stays clean, the content can do the talking.
